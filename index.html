            Import & export

            Paste a playlist link on its own line — a YouTube playlist (youtube.com/playlist?list=…), a SoundCloud set (soundcloud.com/artist/sets/…) or a public Spotify playlist (open.spotify.com/playlist/…). You can also paste many song links, one per line, or the names of songs.

            YouTube playlists and Spotify imports need your free API key; SoundCloud sets need your client ID. Both are in Customise ⚙. Spotify's own catalogue can't be streamed by other apps, so Spotify playlists are matched to YouTube versions of each song.
